Elizabeth Maweu · L&D Performance Toolkit

From workplace problem to measurable performance.

Interactive tools built around a repeatable L&D method: Discovery → Diagnosis → Design → Facilitate & Coach → Impact. The aim is not to prescribe training automatically, but to identify the intervention most likely to change performance.
