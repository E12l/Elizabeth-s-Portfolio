Impact

Discovery

Interviewed employer, marketing, and verification teams and found there was no shared, data-driven way to prioritise outreach.

Diagnosis

Untargeted employer visits were burning resources and slowing verification turnaround. A standard metric was needed to prioritise effort.

Design

Collected 550+ data points over six months and developed a responsiveness score across three tiers (High, Medium, Low), each with a clear recommended action.

Facilitate & Coach

Worked with cross-functional teams to embed the metric into operations reviews and marketing planning, training five team leads and eight verification agents on how to use it.

Impact
